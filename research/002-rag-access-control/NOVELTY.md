# Novelty assessment — CAI-002

The underlying class is established or anticipated by the [cited prior work](SOURCES.md). This package does not claim a new vulnerability class or globally novel attack.

## Contribution made here

A self-contained local system, a six-scenario corpus, three enforcement profiles, actual effect traces, six behavioral regression methods, and a claim register operationalize a narrower boundary: A derived artifact must be authorized at serving time against its current source permissions, not only against the permissions used when creating it.

## What needs novelty review

A future claim needs an implementation/version survey, comparable published baselines, evidence of a previously unreported failure, and independent reproduction. Demonstrating a deliberately seeded omission is insufficient. The earlier repository AIA-001 study already investigates authority gaps; these packages refine specific workflows rather than superseding its findings.

**Novelty status:** local explanatory artifact; global originality and new product flaws not established.
