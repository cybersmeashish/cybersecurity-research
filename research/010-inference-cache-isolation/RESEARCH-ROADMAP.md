# Research roadmap — CAI-010

## Completed local phase

Source-linked threat model, executable seeded/control profiles, 36 fixtures, 108 condition trials, six behavioral regression methods, raw traces and results, source hashes, and bounded conclusions.

## Next phase

Use a scoped inference deployment with real scheduling/noise, trusted gateway identities, fresh held-out prefixes, and a pre-registered sample-size calculation.

## Required acceptance evidence

An independently selected implementation and legitimate workload; explicit policy/target scope; effects verified beyond model wording; failed and missing-context cases preserved; mitigation evaluated for utility and overhead; independent reproduction where possible.

## Publication gate

A real vulnerability claim needs reproducible implementation evidence and coordinated disclosure. A negative result remains publishable with exact versions and conditions. The existing local paper is not promoted to a provider finding by editing metadata.
