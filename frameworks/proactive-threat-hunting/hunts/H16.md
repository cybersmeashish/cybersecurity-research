# H16: Unapproved recovery protection change

## Hypothesis

A recovery/backup change has an explicit unapproved-change context.

**Telemetry:** backup. **Baseline required:** False. **Temporal association:** False.

**Selected technique context:** T1490. Mapping depends on actual observed behavior; this is not adversary attribution.

## Execute

Use the [tested local SQL](../queries/H16.sql) with this framework's normalized schema and [lab runner](../lab/README.md). No native SIEM/provider query is claimed validated. Field requirements are in [hunt-pack.json](../hunt-pack.json).

## Corroborate

Owner/change evidence, actual recovery configuration, surviving copies, independent audit record and restore feasibility.

## Benign alternatives

Retention expiration, controlled restoration, decommissioning or ticket enrichment delay.

## Disposition

Record raw evidence references, source health, identity/tenant boundaries, baseline scope, alternative explanations and analyst confidence. Escalate corroborated leads through the incident process; absence of a result is limited to observed data. Missing coverage yields an inconclusive assessment.
