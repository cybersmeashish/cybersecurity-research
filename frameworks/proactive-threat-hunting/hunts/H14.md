# H14: AI retrieval or tool action outside logged policy scope

## Hypothesis

An application policy observation reports an operation outside approved actor/document/tool scope.

**Telemetry:** ai. **Baseline required:** False. **Temporal association:** False.

**Selected technique context:** No exact ATT&CK mapping asserted. Mapping depends on actual observed behavior; this is not adversary attribution.

## Execute

Use the [tested local SQL](../queries/H14.sql) with this framework's normalized schema and [lab runner](../lab/README.md). No native SIEM/provider query is claimed validated. Field requirements are in [hunt-pack.json](../hunt-pack.json).

## Corroborate

Verified actor, document/chunk/tool scope, policy version and deterministic authorization result; a model label is not sufficient evidence.

## Benign alternatives

Synthetic tests, an incorrect entitlement mapping or a stale policy version.

## Disposition

Record raw evidence references, source health, identity/tenant boundaries, baseline scope, alternative explanations and analyst confidence. Escalate corroborated leads through the incident process; absence of a result is limited to observed data. Missing coverage yields an inconclusive assessment. See [case record](../templates/case-record.md) and [triage](../docs/TRIAGE.md).
