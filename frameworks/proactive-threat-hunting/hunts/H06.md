# H06: Suspicious email click followed by new-device sign-in

## Hypothesis

A suspicious click and new-device sign-in share verified principal context and a bounded time association.

**Telemetry:** email, identity. **Baseline required:** True. **Temporal association:** True.

**Selected technique context:** T1078. Mapping depends on actual observed behavior; this is not adversary attribution.

## Execute

Use the [tested local SQL](../queries/H06.sql) with this framework's normalized schema and [lab runner](../lab/README.md). No native SIEM/provider query is claimed validated. Field requirements are in [hunt-pack.json](../hunt-pack.json).

## Corroborate

Human versus scanner click, URL verdict provenance, raw sign-in session, device and user corroboration.

## Benign alternatives

Security-scanner clicks, legitimate travel/device change or coincidental events.

## Disposition

Record raw evidence references, source health, identity/tenant boundaries, baseline scope, alternative explanations and analyst confidence. Escalate corroborated leads through the incident process; absence of a result is limited to observed data. Missing coverage yields an inconclusive assessment.
