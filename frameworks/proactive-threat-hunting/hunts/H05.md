# H05: Unapproved external mailbox forwarding

## Hypothesis

An unapproved external forwarding rule may enable collection outside the organization.

**Telemetry:** email. **Baseline required:** False. **Temporal association:** False.

**Selected technique context:** T1114.003. Mapping depends on actual observed behavior; this is not adversary attribution.

## Execute

Use the [tested local SQL](../queries/H05.sql) with this framework's normalized schema and [lab runner](../lab/README.md). No native SIEM/provider query is claimed validated. Field requirements are in [hunt-pack.json](../hunt-pack.json).

## Corroborate

Full rule/filter/scope, actual recipients and delivery, change owner, mailbox sessions and approval.

## Benign alternatives

Authorized routing, third-party case management or mailbox migration.

## Disposition

Record raw evidence references, source health, identity/tenant boundaries, baseline scope, alternative explanations and analyst confidence. Escalate corroborated leads through the incident process; absence of a result is limited to observed data. Missing coverage yields an inconclusive assessment.
