# H04: Privileged identity grant outside approved change

## Hypothesis

A privileged grant with an explicit unapproved-change flag deserves review.

**Telemetry:** identity. **Baseline required:** False. **Temporal association:** False.

**Selected technique context:** T1078. Mapping depends on actual observed behavior; this is not adversary attribution.

## Execute

Use the [tested local SQL](../queries/H04.sql) with this framework's normalized schema and [lab runner](../lab/README.md). No native SIEM/provider query is claimed validated. Field requirements are in [hunt-pack.json](../hunt-pack.json).

## Corroborate

Grant scope/expiry, authoritative approval record, grantee activity and effective role evidence.

## Benign alternatives

PIM activation, emergency access, approved onboarding or incorrect ticket enrichment.

## Disposition

Record raw evidence references, source health, identity/tenant boundaries, baseline scope, alternative explanations and analyst confidence. Escalate corroborated leads through the incident process; absence of a result is limited to observed data. Missing coverage yields an inconclusive assessment.
