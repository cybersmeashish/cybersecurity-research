# H13: Large SaaS export through first-observed application

## Hypothesis

A large export by an application absent from the supplied actor baseline warrants consent/data-scope review.

**Telemetry:** saas. **Baseline required:** True. **Temporal association:** False.

**Selected technique context:** No exact ATT&CK mapping asserted. Mapping depends on actual observed behavior; this is not adversary attribution.

## Execute

Use the [tested local SQL](../queries/H13.sql) with this framework's normalized schema and [lab runner](../lab/README.md). No native SIEM/provider query is claimed validated. Field requirements are in [hunt-pack.json](../hunt-pack.json).

## Corroborate

Application consent/scope, source/destination ownership, effective actor rights, business approval and actual exported objects.

## Benign alternatives

Approved reporting, eDiscovery, customer migration or baseline onboarding gaps.

## Disposition

Record raw evidence references, source health, identity/tenant boundaries, baseline scope, alternative explanations and analyst confidence. Escalate corroborated leads through the incident process; absence of a result is limited to observed data. Missing coverage yields an inconclusive assessment.
