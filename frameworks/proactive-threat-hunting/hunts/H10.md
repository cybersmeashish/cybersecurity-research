# H10: Unapproved privileged Kubernetes workload

## Hypothesis

A workload request with privileged settings lacks an approved-change context.

**Telemetry:** kubernetes. **Baseline required:** False. **Temporal association:** False.

**Selected technique context:** No exact ATT&CK mapping asserted. Mapping depends on actual observed behavior; this is not adversary attribution.

## Corroborate

Audit stage/level, requested pod settings, admission decision, actual running settings and namespace owner.

Authorized DaemonSet, node troubleshooting or a deliberately isolated test.

Escalate corroborated leads through the incident process; absence of a result is limited to observed data. Missing coverage yields an inconclusive assessment.
